# Third-party assets

## Farm & Ranch Life

Selected sprites from **Farm & Ranch Life – 16x16 Asset Pack**, by the creator at [michelclauwaertgmailcom.itch.io](https://michelclauwaertgmailcom.itch.io/farm-ranch-life-free-16x16-top-down-pixel-art-asset-pack).

Used for the production machine and delivery shipping bin. Three source sheets, README and the original LICENSE are preserved under `Assets/_Game/Art/Features/Economy/FarmRanchLife/`; the rest of the pack is not included. The creator discloses AI assistance.

The included license permits use/modification in personal and commercial projects as part of a larger work, and prohibits standalone asset redistribution, claiming the art as original work, NFT use and AI training. See the included LICENSE.txt for full terms.
